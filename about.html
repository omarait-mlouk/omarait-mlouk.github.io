About Me

Hey, I'm Omar! I'm a Master's student in AI and Data Science at Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Ouarzazate, Morocco, where I'm conducting advanced research in computer vision and deep learning. My passion lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and practical applications that solve real-world problems.

I've been building AI solutions and developing software since my undergraduate years, and I believe in the power of open-source collaboration and knowledge sharing. Welcome to my corner of the internet where I document my journey, share what I learn, and showcase the projects I'm passionate about.

Current Focus

I'm currently working on my Master's thesis focusing on DeblurGAN-v2, exploring advanced motion deblurring techniques using Feature Pyramid Networks and Relativistic GANs. This research aims to achieve significant efficiency gains while maintaining high-quality image restoration.

Alongside my research, I'm developing practical applications including tourism booking systems for Morocco, demonstrating how AI and modern web technologies can enhance traditional industries.
