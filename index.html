About Me

AI Engineer · Deep Learning Researcher · Full-Stack Developer

Hi, I’m Omar Ait Mlouk 👋

I’m a Master’s student in Artificial Intelligence and Applications at Université Ibn Zohr in Morocco, while working as a Full-Stack Developer and Python & Odoo ERP Specialist at AinnovateX.

With over 4 years of professional experience in software development, I’ve had the privilege of working on diverse projects ranging from enterprise ERP solutions to cutting-edge computer vision systems. My journey in tech began with web development and gradually evolved into the fascinating world of AI and machine learning.

What I Do

I specialize in building end-to-end AI solutions, from research and model development to deployment and optimization. My recent work includes:

  • Implementing state-of-the-art deep learning models like DeblurGAN-v2 for image restoration
  • Developing computer vision systems for medical imaging and character recognition
  • Creating full-stack web applications with modern frameworks
  • Building custom ERP solutions and business automation tools

Research Interests

My research focuses on:

  • Computer Vision: Image restoration, GANs, and medical imaging
  • Deep Learning: Neural architecture design and optimization
  • NLP: Multilingual systems and low-resource languages
  • MLOps: Model deployment and production systems
